# e-mahata Naftal Portal — Visual Direction

## Ground-truth reference

This implementation follows the supplied e-mahata Naftal portal brief as the visual ground truth: an Arabic, right-to-left official service portal with a white-and-deep-blue authority-led header, Naftal yellow action highlights, a composed service-first hero, a high-utility tyre-reservation form, and reassuring compliance content. The page should feel like a trusted public digital service, not a promotional consumer landing page.

## Chosen Approach: Official Service Gateway

### Design Movement

Contemporary public-sector digital service design, combining formal Algerian corporate identity cues with the clarity and task focus of a national e-services portal.

### Core Principles

1. **Task-first hierarchy:** the reservation tool must be the clearest visual priority after the opening statement.
2. **Measured authority:** deep Naftal blue, clean white surfaces, purposeful rules, and strong information grouping convey reliability.
3. **Arabic-native rhythm:** generous Arabic line heights, RTL-aligned navigation, and right-anchored informational accents make the language the interface rather than a translation layer.
4. **Friendly precision:** yellow accents identify important actions and status while restrained cards and shadows keep the environment calm and accountable.

### Color Philosophy

The interface uses Naftal blue as a stable institutional anchor and Naftal yellow as a high-visibility signal for actions, active states, and assurance marks. Pale blue-grey backgrounds distinguish service areas without competing with form controls, while white provides visual breathing room and readable data-entry surfaces.

### Layout Paradigm

A horizontal utility bar and navigation frame a broad hero band. The booking tool partially overlaps the lower hero boundary as a primary operational object, creating a clear transition from explanation to action. Supporting content follows as a sequence of long, right-aligned content strips rather than a generic card wall.

### Signature Elements

1. A yellow directional stripe and diamond-shaped brand mark that repeat in the header, hero, and key actions.
2. Fine blue route-line motifs evocative of the nationwide station network.
3. Deep-blue service panels with one decisive yellow primary action.

### Interaction Philosophy

Every interaction should confirm a clear next step: dropdowns open compactly, selected choices use blue or yellow indicators, and calls to action gently highlight their intended workflow. Changes are direct and never playful or distracting.

### Animation

Hover and selection states transition in 160–220 ms using a crisp ease-out curve. The booking panel and steps may fade upward slightly on initial load, with motion disabled for reduced-motion preferences. Active controls use a subtle press scale and button hover lift only.

### Typography System

Use **Tajawal** for Arabic display and body text, with bold weights for page titles and semi-bold labels. Arabic headings use tight visual grouping and increased line height; utility text uses a smaller, quieter size. French-language microcopy uses the same family for coherence.

### Brand Essence

**e-mahata is Naftal’s trusted digital gateway for planning tyre collection and locating essential services across Algeria.**

Personality: **reliable, direct, civic-minded**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are concise and service-oriented; CTAs tell the visitor exactly what action will follow; microcopy removes uncertainty in plain Arabic.

Examples:

> «احجز عجلاتك واختر مركز الاستلام الذي يناسبك.»

> «أدخل مواصفات العجلات للعثور على الخيارات المتاحة.»

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark combines a confident blue road-line/energy droplet symbol with a small yellow directional accent. The e-mahata wordmark is placed alongside the Arabic Naftal name in bold Arabic lettering, expressing a direct link between the trusted corporate identity and its new digital service.

### Signature Brand Color

**Naftal Signal Yellow — #FFC20E**
